## 🎨 100% Light-Only Executive Color Palette

The entire design system has been migrated to a luminous, modern, light aesthetic:
- **Base Canvas Background**: `#FAFBFD` (Soft Pearl White) & `#FFFFFF` (Pure Luminous White)
- **Card & Surface Background**: `#FFFFFF` with soft 1px border `#E2E8F0` and shadow `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
- **Primary Brand Gradient**: `#0284C7` (Electric Azure Blue) to `#0D9488` (Teal)
- **Hygiene & Vitality Accent**: `#10B981` (Fresh Emerald Green)
- **Action & Urgency Highlight**: `#F59E0B` (Solar Amber)
- **High-Contrast Typography**: `#0F172A` (Charcoal Slate - AAA contrast ratio 14.5:1 on white) and `#334155` (Slate 700)
- **Subtle Surface Tints**: `#F0F9FF` (Ice Blue Tint) & `#F0FDFA` (Mint Tint)
