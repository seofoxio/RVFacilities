How Radical Vision Facilities evolved from a single mobility contract in 2006 into a consolidated multi-vertical facility partner across Maharashtra.

The RV Journey: Grassroots Grit to Enterprise Scale

Established in 2006 under the leadership of Mr. Rahul Shinde, RV Facilities was founded on a simple yet unyielding principle: corporate facilities and industrial operations should never grind to a halt due to logistical friction, labor non-compliance, or maintenance breakdowns.

Starting with dedicated corporate mobility fleets serving the nascent IT corridors of Hinjewadi and Magarpatta, our track record of punctuality and 100% statutory transparency soon led clients to request broader operational coverage. Over the next decade, we expanded systematically into critical mobile tower erection and O&M for major telecom circles, industrial manpower outsourcing, and mechanized campus housekeeping.

On 24 April 2024, to support expanded multi-location client engagements, we incorporated formally as Radical Vision Facilities Pvt. Ltd., integrating our proprietary Harmony Cloud ERP to give facility directors real-time billing, attendance, and compliance audits with zero friction.

"Facility management is not about outsourcing headcount; it is about providing enterprise clients complete peace of mind. When an MNC or telecom conglomerate partners with RV, they gain 100% statutory legal immunity, 24/7 accountability, and a team that takes ownership of ground reality."

— Rahul Shinde • CEO & Founder, Radical Vision Facilities Pvt. Ltd.
